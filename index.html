🔤 Word Formation Masterclass

Learn how to recognize syntactic clues, then apply your knowledge!

📌 1. Nouns (Danh từ - N)

Where? After articles (a/an/the), possessives (my/his/Minh's), prepositions (in/on/of), or adjectives.

Examples: a collection, his creativity, interested in gardening, a beautiful model.
Common suffixes: -tion, -ity, -er, -ness, -ment

📌 2. Verbs (Động từ - V)

Where? After a subject, after "to" (to-infinitive), or modals (can/will/should).

Examples: He collects, want to create, can benefit.
Common suffixes: -ate, -en, -ize

📌 3. Adjectives (Tính từ - Adj)

Where? Before a noun, or after "to be" (is/are) and linking verbs (look/feel).

Examples: an unusual hobby, is very popular, looks patient.
Common suffixes: -al, -ive, -able, -ent, -ful

📌 4. Adverbs (Trạng từ - Adv)

Where? After an action verb to describe how the action is done.

Examples: waited patiently, designed creatively.
Common suffixes: -ly

📐 Grammar Masterclass

Student-centered learning guided by Bloom's Taxonomy principles.

Part A: Present Simple (Deep Dive)

📌 1. Usage (When do we use it?)

  • Habits & Routines: I play football every day.
  • General Truths & Facts: The sun rises in the east.
  • Timetables & Schedules: The train leaves at 8:00 AM tomorrow.

📌 2. Structure

(+) AffirmativeI/You/We/They play
He/She/It plays
(-) NegativeI/You/We/They don't play
He/She/It doesn't play
(?) QuestionDo I/you/we/they play?
Does he/she/it play?

📌 3. Spelling Rules (for He/She/It)

RuleExamples
Most verbs: add -swork → works, read → reads
Ends in -o, -sh, -ch, -x, -ss: add -esgo → goes, wash → washes, watch → watches
Consonant + -y: change to -iesstudy → studies, fly → flies

📌 4. Time Signals (Adverbs of Frequency)

always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never

Note: They come BEFORE normal verbs (He always plays) but AFTER the 'be' verb (He is always happy).

Apply the rules to conjugate the verbs correctly.
